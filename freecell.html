Double-Deck FreeCell

Two shuffled decks — 104 cards — dealt face up across ten columns, with eight free cells and eight foundations. Build each foundation up by suit from Ace to King; two foundations fill with each suit. Build the columns down in alternating colours, and put any card on an empty column. A run moves only if there is room to shuffle it one card at a time: (free cells + 1) × 2empty columns cards, halved when the empty column is the one you are moving to. Cards may be taken back off the foundations at any time. Click a card to send it to the best available spot, or drag it where you want it. Undo is unlimited — you can walk a game all the way back to the deal.
