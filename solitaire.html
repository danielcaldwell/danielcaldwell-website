Klondike Solitaire

Moves 0 Time 0:00
You win!

Draw one card at a time — click the stock to turn a card, and click it again once empty to recycle the waste. Build the four foundations up by suit from Ace to King, and build the columns down in alternating colours. House rule: any card or run may be moved onto an empty column, not just a King. Click a card to send it to the best available spot, or drag it where you want it.
